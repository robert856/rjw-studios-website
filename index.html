About

A designer’s eye. A craftsman’s hands.

RJW Studios is the independent design and woodworking studio of Robert Williams. With more than 26 years of experience, Robert creates custom furniture, cabinetry, and architectural millwork guided by thoughtful proportions, beautiful materials, and lasting craftsmanship.

His career includes serving as Vice President of Design and Merchandising at Habersham and Senior Director of Design at Ballard Designs. At Habersham, he designed licensed furniture collections for Biltmore Estate, Hearst Castle, and The Plaza Hotel, along with a Monet collection licensed through the Versailles Foundation.

Travels through museums, historic homes, and grand estates around the world have shaped his appreciation for architecture and decorative detail. These influences inform his work, interpreted for the way people live today.

At RJW Studios, Robert works directly with clients to create distinctive furniture, fitted cabinetry, and millwork that bring character and purpose to their homes.
