About

Design experience, brought to the workshop

RJW Studios is the design and fabrication practice of Robert Williams, a furniture designer and craftsman with more than 26 years of experience and 300+ custom pieces built by hand.

Robert previously served as Vice President of Design and Merchandising at Habersham and Senior Director of Product Design at Ballard Designs, and has developed licensed furniture collections for high-profile historic properties.

RJW Studios brings that same executive-level design discipline to every custom cabinetry, furniture, and millwork commission — designed, engineered, and finished under one roof.
